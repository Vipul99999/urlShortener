# Roadmap

This is the short roadmap document for ongoing product planning.

For the fuller PRD and roadmap, use:

## Current Product Level

UrlShortener is currently in the strong startup-product stage:

- real feature depth
- usable frontend
- multi-tenant model
- custom domains
- analytics
- exports
- scoped API keys
- worker-based async processing

## Near-Term Priorities

- polish live deployment validation
- grow browser E2E coverage
- improve abuse review surfaces
- keep onboarding and first-run UX sharp

## Mid-Term Priorities

- better reporting and analytics filters
- scheduled exports
- more operational dashboards
- stronger domain onboarding guidance

## Later Priorities

- billing and entitlements
- more advanced team controls
- richer integrations and webhooks
- deeper analytics infrastructure

## Product Principle

Do not overbuild too early.

The right direction is:

1. excellent startup-stage product quality
2. operational trust
3. better growth and monetization layers later
